How it works

Pavilion Cerebro is the brain.

Agents are the senses. The coordinator is the brain. Every agent speaks the same language, so adding a new building system means adding one more agent, not rebuilding the platform.

  1. 1

    Sense

    Each agent watches one system: sensor feeds, BMS points, camera and drone images, inspection records.

  2. 2

    Think

    The coordinator fuses every report into one building risk picture and ranks it by risk × consequence × time.

  3. 3

    Act

    Past a threshold it acts: isolates a circuit, closes a valve, throttles a battery, pages the right person.

  4. 4

    Heal

    It confirms the fix held, logs the cost and updates the building's health score.
