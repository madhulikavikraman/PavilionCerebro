Prompt D: How can we detect infrastructure damage and failures before they become expensive, and rapidly prioritize recovery when they occur?

Utilities, telecommunications companies, transportation networks, insurers, industrial operators, and infrastructure owners spend billions inspecting, maintaining, and repairing physical assets.
Much of this work remains manual and reactive. Organizations send employees into the field to inspect assets, analyze imagery manually, and respond after equipment has already failed. Wildfires, earthquakes, floods, storms, and other disasters amplify this problem by requiring organizations to assess thousands of assets while every hour of downtime creates additional economic losses.


At the same time, satellites, drones, connected vehicles, smartphones, cameras, and sensors are producing unprecedented amounts of physical-world data.

Explore solutions using AI, computer vision, geospatial intelligence, robotics, sensors, or other technologies to identify infrastructure deterioration and damage, predict failures, automate inspections, and prioritize maintenance and recovery resources.

The strongest solutions should address everyday inspection, maintenance, or asset-management workflows, with disasters providing an additional high-value application.
